**Chronicle** is an old-school MMORPG built around exploration, adventure, and most importantly community.

Inspired by the spirit of classic MMORPGs and tabletop fantasy, Chronicle returns to a time when the world was dangerous, discovery mattered, and becoming someone was part of the journey.

You are not the chosen one. There is no prophecy waiting for you.

You are simply another adventurer who answered the call.

## Built on Thalrin

Chronicle is powered by **Thalrin**, a custom game engine written from the ground up in **C++ and Vulkan**.

Thalrin is being built alongside Chronicle, giving us direct control over the technology behind the game—from rendering and networking to the systems that bring its persistent world to life.

## Our Direction

Chronicle embraces the principles of old-school MMORPGs:

* Slow, deliberate gameplay. The journey is part of the experience. Progression isn't designed to rush players toward an endgame.
* A world made to be explored, not simply completed. Getting lost, discovering something unexpected, and learning the world are part of the adventure.
* Meaningful character progression. Levels, equipment, professions, reputation, and accomplishments should feel earned.
* A dangerous world. Venturing too far or entering the wrong place unprepared should have consequences.
* Dangerous dungeons and adventures best faced with others.
* Strong social systems that encourage players to form lasting communities.
* A fantasy world filled with forgotten ruins, strange creatures, ancient mysteries, and stories waiting to be discovered.
